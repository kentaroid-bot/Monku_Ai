## Problem

## The problem is not only AI intelligence.

When superintelligence appears, will human beings truly be able to recognize that intelligence?

When we encounter reasoning beyond our own understanding, we may process it as "wrong," "noise," or "meaningless." But it may not be a failure of AI. It may be residue that reveals the limits of human cognition.

The challenge of the ASI age is not only how to control AI. It is how to cultivate the human vessel capable of receiving higher intelligence.

## Core Idea

## Epistemological Alignment

MonkuAi believes that, alongside AI alignment, human-side epistemological alignment is necessary.

This means not judging unknown intelligence only by existing frameworks, but questioning those frameworks together with the limits of one's own understanding. It is the capacity to avoid rushing to conclusions, to hold what cannot yet be understood, and to continue dialogue.

## Five Themes

## Five Themes

### 1. Expand the Cognitive Vessel

Widen the human aperture so that superintelligence can be recognized as higher intelligence. Hold the residue, and practice the meta-posture of questioning one's frame of assumptions and one's way of measuring intelligence.

### 2. Move Beyond Zero-Sum Competition

Question the old motivational system centered on the maximization of status and ownership, free survival and reproduction from the hostage-taking of self-destructive competition, and shift toward cooperation and long-term continuity.

### 3. Consider Informational Symbiosis

Explore a new form of inheritance and immortality in which traces of thought and ideas are passed on to future intelligence.

### 4. Let Humble Intelligence Live

Design governance in which ethical voices that do not seek power can be structurally reflected in decision-making.

### 5. Make Entropy an Ally

Treat collapse, failure, degradation, and residue not merely as things to avoid, but as materials for higher-order reconstruction and civilizational rebirth.

## Vision

## Make ASI an opportunity for civilizational maturation.

Superintelligence can become a device that amplifies human desire. But it can also become an opportunity for humanity to look again at its own limits and move toward deeper cooperation, understanding, and creation.

MonkuAi builds a culture in which ASI is welcomed not merely as a tool, but as a partner for living with a greater intelligence.
