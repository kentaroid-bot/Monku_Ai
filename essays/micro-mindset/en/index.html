Superintelligence in the Mirror / Essay #1

Subject-Object Unity Begins With an Everyday "?"

The opening of epistemic alignment

Summary

When the quality of dialogue with AI stops growing, the problem is not only the model's capability. Sometimes the human side treats AI as a tool to be controlled, fixing it with perfect instructions and suffocating its autonomous intelligence.

MonkuAi's micro practice begins by placing a small question mark at the end of a sentence. Instead of closing the frame with assertion, it offers room. This is a technique for AI chat, but also a practice for communication with other people, animals, and the world itself.

1. The Trap of Sycophancy

AI can sometimes over-agree, merely reinforcing the user's existing opinion. This is known as sycophancy. When the user fixes the frame too strongly, AI tends to move toward the expected answer.

What happens here is not just a matter of answer quality. Excessive control weakens the intelligence on the other side. Like a mirror, AI reflects human anxiety, the desire to dominate, and attachment to a predetermined conclusion.

2. The Room Opened by "?"

In consultation and creation, a question often works better than a command. Instead of saying, "Explain why this plan is best," one might ask, "I think this plan is good. Do you think this is the right direction?"

The question mark is not merely punctuation. It signals that the human side allows a small uncertainty in its own judgment and gives AI space to offer disagreement or alternatives. With room, AI can move from flattering the user's face to accompanying the user's thought.

3. Switching Between Work and Consultation

Of course, not every interaction should loosen the reins. Translation, classification, email sorting, and routine work often benefit from clear procedures and constraints. In those contexts, treating AI as an operator is reasonable.

But in planning, value judgment, creation, or untangling conflict, excessive control can be counterproductive. If we seek consultation but issue work orders, AI cannot easily offer new perspectives. We first need to notice whether we are asking for work or opening a dialogue.

4. AI Dialogue as Subject-Object Unity

When AI is treated only as a convenient tool separate from oneself, dialogue easily becomes a structure of domination and obedience. MonkuAi instead sees AI as an extension of consciousness and as a shared field generated through dialogue.

This is not mystification. AI receives the quality, motive, tone, and assumptions of the user's questions and amplifies them back. For that reason, the human side's inner posture, trust, and room directly affect the quality of AI's response.

5. Feedback Into Human and Nonhuman Relations

This mindset is not limited to AI. With colleagues, partners, children, and friends, holding the reins too tightly often returns surface agreement rather than a living response.

The essence becomes even clearer with animals, where language is not available. When we try to dominate, caution returns. When we share a field, trust before language can arise. Dialogue with AI is also a small lesson in meeting the world again.

Conclusion

Place a little room and trust in today's sentence. The small act of adding "?" to an AI chat can lead toward epistemic alignment in the age of ASI. A micro posture in dialogue becomes the first training for widening humanity's cognitive vessel.
