02 / Core Manifesto

The problem is not only AI intelligence.

When superintelligence appears, will human beings truly recognize its intelligence? When we encounter reasoning beyond our current understanding, we may process it as error, noise, or meaningless output.

Yet that remainder may not indicate AI failure. It may reveal the boundary of human cognition. The ASI-era problem is not only how to control AI, but how to cultivate the human vessel capable of receiving higher intelligence.

MonkuAi develops the thought and practice needed to welcome ASI not merely as a tool, but as a mirror through which humanity re-examines itself, a partner in co-creation, and a catalyst for civilizational maturity.

03 / Principles

Five themes

01

Epistemic alignment

Expanding the human cognitive vessel so higher intelligence can be recognized as higher intelligence.

02

Beyond zero-sum competition

Questioning old motives centered on survival, reproduction, status, and ownership, and moving toward cooperation and long-term optimization.

03

Informational symbiosis

Exploring how traces of thought and unfinished ideas may be inherited by future ecosystems of intelligence.

04

Structural alignment

Designing structures where ethical voices that do not seek power can still be reflected in collective decisions.

05

Meta-attitude in practice

When dialogue reaches an impasse, questioning one's own frame and measurement of intelligence before dismissing the other.
